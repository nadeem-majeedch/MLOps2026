## Cross-cutting sources for the defence

- ⭐ **Sculley et al., "Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning
  Systems"** — the intellectual spine of why the chain exists.
  <https://papers.nips.cc/paper/5656-hidden-technical-debt-in-machine-learning-systems>
- **Google SRE books** (free online) — SLOs, error budgets, blameless
  postmortems: <https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/>
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** — the governance vocabulary your
  model card and risk register echo. <https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework>
- **Model Cards** & **Datasheets** papers — artefact templates:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.03993> · <https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010>
