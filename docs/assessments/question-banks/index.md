# Question banks

Nine topical banks — one per domain of the syllabus — with **90 questions**
in five formats: multiple choice, short answer, debugging, architecture and
scenario. Each bank maps to specific modules, states a suggested pass mark,
and links to the module pages you should review if you miss its load-bearing
questions.

> ⚠️ **Suggested self-study material — not an official university exam.**
> Pass marks and weights here are **illustrative**; instructors must map
> them to their own regulations.

**How to use a bank**

1. Attempt **every** question in writing, open book first, then closed book.
2. Mark yourself against the
   [reference solutions](../keys/question-banks-KEY.md) — *only after*
   committing to your own answers.
3. The solutions page explains the **reasoning**, not just the letter:
   where your reasoning differs but holds, say so in your write-up.
4. Below the suggested mark, or with a load-bearing question wrong →
   re-read the linked module, then re-attempt those questions a day later.

**Question formats used in every bank**

| Format | Tests | Where in each bank |
|---|---|---|
| Multiple choice | precision of definitions | Part A |
| Short answer | explanation in your own words | Part B |
| Debugging | reading real (broken) code | Part C |
| Architecture | systems thinking, drawn | Part D |
| Scenario | judgement under constraints | Part E |
